/Questions

Before you apply.

What does the beta actually include?

Everything in Pro: the market map, competitor teardown, gap analysis and 90-day plan, plus the daily market update and read-only data connections. Nothing is redacted and nothing is a demo. If you want to see the shape of the output first, the public specimen is a real deliverable.

How long does beta access last?

Seven days or seven full reports, whichever runs out first. The clock starts the moment your application is approved — not when you submit it — so nothing is burned while you wait.

How many reports can I actually run?

Two full reports per rolling seven days, on any company you like, plus one short daily update per day. Because of that cadence the seven-report ceiling is the looser of the two bounds — a beta week is realistically two full reports and a handful of daily updates.

Do I need to enter a card?

No. The beta takes no payment details and does not roll into a subscription. When the window closes your account drops back to Free, and going Pro at £20/month is a decision you make separately.

What happens to my reports when it ends?

Every report you generated stays readable in your workspace permanently. Only the ability to generate new ones locks.

I applied — how will I know?

Your status shows on this page and in your workspace the moment it changes. There is no queue position to check and no chasing required.

How is this different from the waitlist?

The waitlist is a marketing list for general launch news and Agentic interest — being on it grants nothing and it is not connected to your account. This page is the only route to actual product access.

Who is this not for?

Pre-idea founders without a product, and enterprises with an in-house market-intelligence team. The engine assumes you have something in market and can judge whether its read on your competitors is right. If that is not you yet, the methodology is worth reading anyway.

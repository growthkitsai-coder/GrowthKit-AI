01 — Who we are

Who we are.

"You" means any individual or entity that visits our website, joins our waitlist, communicates with us, or otherwise uses our services (the "Services").

What this policy covers.

This policy applies to personal information we collect through our website, waitlist forms, email correspondence, and any pre-release or beta features we may offer. It does not apply to third-party websites, products, or services, even if they link to or from ours.

By accessing or using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Services.

03 — Information collected

Information we collect.

Information you provide

  • Contact details — your name, email address, company name, role, and any other details you submit through the waitlist, contact forms, or email.
  • Communications — the contents of messages you send us, including questions, feedback, and any attachments.
  • Pilot & beta data — if you participate in a pilot or private beta, information about your company, growth stage, channels, and goals as you choose to share it.

Information collected automatically

  • Device & usage data — IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, links clicked, and timestamps.
  • Cookies & similar technologies — see Section 07.

Information from third parties

We may receive limited information about you from analytics providers, hosting providers, and publicly available sources (for example, your professional profile if you contact us through LinkedIn).

04 — Use

How we use information.

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Services;
  • Respond to inquiries, manage waitlist access, and deliver pilot communications;
  • Understand how the Services are used and develop new features;
  • Detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, security, or technical issues;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms; and
  • Send you transactional or, where permitted, marketing communications (you can opt out of marketing at any time).

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your personal information to train third-party large language models without your consent.

Legal basis (UK/EEA).

If you are in the United Kingdom or European Economic Area, we process personal data under one or more of the following legal bases: (a) your consent; (b) performance of a contract with you; (c) compliance with a legal obligation; or (d) our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the Services, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.

We share personal information only as described below:

  • With your consent — for any other purpose disclosed to you at the time.

# Pages — every HTML file in the repo

> Part of the GrowthKit AI docs set. Read [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md) first — it holds the non-negotiable mandates and conventions. This file is the deep reference for what each page is and contains. **If you add, remove, or materially change a page, update this file** (and follow the new-page checklist in [`docs/infrastructure.md`](infrastructure.md)).

All pages live at the repo root. Every public page is self-contained: inline `<style>` in `<head>`, shared `theme.css`/`theme.js`, duplicated topbar/footer (no templating). Dark-mode treatments referenced below are detailed in [`docs/design-system.md`](design-system.md).

- **`four.html`** (`/four`) — **the gated product dashboard and GrowthKit Live workspace. Rebuilt as a two-mode app shell 2026-07-27.**
  - **Its `.auth-top` bar carries the full canonical subpage nav (added 2026-07-27)** — Product · How it works · Methodology · Beta · Contact, byte-identical to the other 12 subpages. Until then /four had **no route back into the site at all**: login/signup/reset each have a "← Back to site" link, this page had only the logo. The nav **stays visible in workspace mode** and hides under 720px, matching the public topbars. It is the only auth page with nav; the others keep their back link. Base `.nav-links` rules live in `auth.css` and are **deliberately unscoped** — `theme.css` styles the Beta pill at `.nav-links a.nav-beta` (0,2,1), and since `auth.css` loads after `theme.css`, an `.auth-top .nav-links a` rule would tie on specificity and strip the Beta link's accent colour.
  - **Before the first completed report** it is the marketing scroll it has always been: `.four-welcome` hero (cycling typewriter greeting), three-step `[data-guide]`, the engine, the `[data-specimen]` card, beta and Pro cards. Free accounts can complete the saved adaptive wizard and see the locked preview.
  - **The specimen is a card, not an inline frame (changed 2026-07-27).** `[data-specimen]` renders `.four-specimen-card` — title, section summary, a `[data-specimen-open]` button and an "open in a new tab" link. The button opens `.four-specimen-overlay`, a full-screen dialog (`z-index: 1000`, backdrop + `.four-specimen-bar` with a close button) whose iframe **has no `src` until first open**, then loads **`/specimen?embed=1`**. Closes on the ✕, the backdrop, or Escape — including Escape pressed inside the iframe, which `product.js` wires into `frame.contentDocument` on load because key events don't bubble out of a frame. The overlay lives **after `</main>`** so no ancestor transform can break its `position: fixed` and pane switching can't hide it; `html.gk-specimen-open` locks page scroll. **It was previously an always-on 560px `<iframe src="/specimen">`** — which both trapped the wheel mid-scroll and, missing `?embed=1`, rendered the specimen's own topbar (a second Log in / Join waitlist, wrapping badly at 900px) inside the frame.
  - **Once a completed report exists**, `product.js` adds `.is-workspace` to `<body>` and the page becomes an app shell: a `[data-workspace-head]` header naming the company `/four` follows (with two `.four-meter` readouts — full reports left this week, and today's daily update), a `[data-workspace-nav]` left rail, and six `[data-pane]` panes — **Deliverable · Daily · Plan · Connections · History · Billing**. The welcome hero, guide and specimen hide; the billing bar and status pill relocate into the Billing pane. At ≤720px the rail becomes a fixed bottom tab bar using each item's `data-short` label (`aria-label` carries the full name). Panes deep-link by hash (`/four#daily`).
  - **Cadence:** Pro, Agentic, and current beta accounts get **two full reports per rolling 7 days** on any company, plus **one short daily update per UTC day**. The ten-stage renderer adds TAM/SAM/SOM, ranked segments, market trend and live indexed-demand charts where available, three GTM plays, window-of-opportunity scoring, funding radar/lists, and weekly metrics from every configured Stripe/GA4/LinkedIn connection to newly generated reports; legacy completed reports keep their original layout.
  - The desktop engine is a 1,380px maximum working canvas (48px gutters) in scroll mode; inside the shell the grid supplies the width and the breakout is cancelled. Mobile retains 12px gutters. `[data-allowance]` under the engine reads "Next full report in Xd HH:MM:SS" once the window is spent; the server remains authoritative.
  - The **Plan pane is a projection**: `product.js` *moves* `#gk-report-gaps` and `#gk-report-plan` out of the rendered deliverable on every `gk:deliverable-rendered` event. Moved, not cloned — `findings.js` has bound checklist listeners to those nodes.
  - A `[data-beta]` card handles apply/pending/granted/spent states and stays hidden for paid/private-allowlist accounts. `[data-history]` lists reports newest-first and links to `/four?report_id=…`.
  - `[data-connect-nudge]` is a standing "add your connections" strip at the foot of `[data-panes]`, so it sits under whichever pane is open (and at the end of the pre-report scroll). `product.js` names the providers that are still unconnected and **hides it once all three are connected**, or when the account has no access. Its CTA opens the Connections pane in workspace mode, or scrolls to the panel in scroll mode.
  - Keep `.four-welcome`, `[data-gk-billing]`, `#four-greeting-h`, `[data-gk-advisor]`, and each `[data-pane]` unique: duplicate wrappers cause browser re-parenting and break the layout.
  - `product.js` owns mode/panes/meters/allowance/daily/history/integrations, `advisor.js` owns onboarding and report rendering, `findings.js` owns working-document actions. See [`daily-intelligence.md`](daily-intelligence.md), [`integrations.md`](integrations.md), and [`advisor.md`](advisor.md).
- All four: `noindex`, no footer, minimal top bar; Supabase Auth via CDN; shared `auth.css` + `auth.js` + `auth-config.js`. **Sign-in is inactive until Supabase keys are pasted into `auth-config.js`** (pages show a "not configured" notice until then).
- **`/four` styling invariant:** the print-only finding-task rules in `auth.css` must close their `@media print` block before the welcome, guide, specimen, and Pro-card rules. An unclosed block makes those dashboard cards render as plain text on screen; the site checker now rejects unbalanced CSS.
- **`/four` workspace CSS invariant:** every workspace rule in `auth.css` is scoped to `body.is-workspace`. Nothing in that block may leak into the unscoped selectors above it, or the pre-first-report scroll collapses into a one-pane view for accounts that have never generated anything.

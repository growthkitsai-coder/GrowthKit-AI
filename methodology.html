04What we believe

Opinions we hold strongly.

We're building for a specific kind of founder — one who treats every other part of their company as a system, and is tired of growth being the part that runs on vibes. These are the priors that shape the product.

01 / Believe

Growth should be engineered, not guessed.

The same rigor that produced your roadmap should produce your go‑to‑market plan. Measure the market, dissect the field, name the gaps. Then act.

02 / Believe

Evidence beats opinion.

Every claim we put in front of you is anchored to data we surfaced — demand signals, complaint mining, pricing matrices — not a Twitter thread an investor liked once.

03 / Believe

Plans should produce decisions, not reports.

The output isn't a deck. It's a sequenced 90‑day plan with kill‑criteria — built so it keeps deciding for you in week four when the founder gets distracted.

04 / Reject

Consulting‑grade work shouldn't take consulting‑grade time.

A McKinsey engagement is six months and six figures. We deliver the same caliber of synthesis in 90 seconds of compute and a monthly SaaS price.

05 / Reject

We're not for everyone.

Pre‑idea founders looking for what to build, and enterprises with a fully staffed growth org — this isn't sharpest for you. We're built for seed and Series A teams that have a product and a customer and want to compound both.

05Method & caveats

How to read this document.

Where the numbers come from. Demand signals are scraped from search volumes, hiring posts, community threads, review sites, and competitor traffic estimates, then de-duplicated and scored. Market sizing triangulates public filings, analyst estimates, and bottoms-up buyer counts — when sources disagree, we show the conservative figure.

Segment scores (0–100) weight demand momentum, buyer urgency, competitive crowding, and your credible right-to-win. A 70+ segment deserves a play this quarter; below 40 is a watch-list entry, not a target.

Kill criteria are commitments. Each play names the number and the date that kills it. Honouring them is what makes this a plan instead of a wish — week-four drift is the failure mode this document is designed against.

Every claim is sourced to a signal we collected this refresh. If a claim matters to a decision and you want the underlying data, reply to the delivery email and we'll send the raw extract.

Caveats. Estimates marked “~” carry ±30% uncertainty. Competitor pricing reflects their public pages on the issue date; private/custom pricing may differ. This is decision-support, not investment advice.

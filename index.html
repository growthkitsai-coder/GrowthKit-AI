01The problem

Most early‑stage growth is just vibes. We don't think it has to be.

Founders ship product on a system. Then they ship growth on hope — six tabs of advice, a Notion full of half‑finished launches, a friend's Twitter thread. We rebuilt that part as a methodology.

01

No written thesis

You can't tell us, in two sentences, exactly which user you're for and which one you aren't. So neither can your landing page.

02

No repeatable channel

Wins come from heroic one‑offs, not a system that keeps producing them after the founder stops posting.

03

Slow, expensive feedback

It takes six weeks to learn something a structured experiment could have shown you in three days.

04

Tooling, not strategy

Eleven SaaS subscriptions, no point of view. Tools amplify a strategy; they don't replace one.

02The engine

A complete plan, written by a system that has read the playbooks and the postmortems.

You hand us your product in plain English. We return four documents you can hand to a contractor, an investor, or a co‑founder by Monday.

03How it works

Three steps. One afternoon.

We deliberately kept this short. The point is to get you back to talking to customers, not to keep you in our product.

i.

Brief us in plain English

Paste your site, your deck, or three paragraphs about what you're building and who it's for.

~ 5 minutes of your time
ii.

We synthesize the thesis

Our system pulls comparable launches, demand signals, and channel economics into a draft you can argue with.

~ 90 seconds of compute
iii.

You review & ship

Edit, sign off, and run the 90‑day plan. We track outcomes weekly so you can see what's working without writing a report.

05Questions

Sensible objections, answered.

If you don't see yours, write to info@growthkitai.com. A founder, not a form, will reply.

Q.01
Is this an agency, or software?
Software, with operator review. The plan is generated by our system; a partner from our team reviews it before it lands in your inbox. You don't pay agency rates and you don't get agency lead times.
+
Q.02
Who is this not for?
Pre‑idea founders looking for what to build, and large enterprises with a fully staffed growth org. We're sharpest for seed and Series A teams that have a product and a customer and want to compound both.
+
Q.03
How is this different from prompting an LLM yourself?
We've fed the system a curated corpus of launch postmortems, demand data, and channel economics, and wrapped it in evaluators that catch the usual generic answers. The output is tied to artifacts you can ship, not advice you have to translate.
+
Q.04
What does it cost?
Our basic pilot provides founders with limited access. Standard plans start at $40/month after that. For more advanced plans for 1‑to‑1 aid from experts in growth, contact us directly.
+
Q.05
Who is behind it?
A small team of founders, engineers, and growth operators who got tired of watching good products fail at the same predictable steps. We share more on the methodology page.

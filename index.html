01The problem

Most founders go to market on hunches.
We don't think they should have to.

Founders ship product with a roadmap, then ship growth with a feeling — a vague sense of market size, a glance at three competitors, an instinct about what to do next. We turn that fog into intelligence: the market measured, the field dissected, the gaps named. The kind of work a strategy consultancy would charge you six figures for. Without the six‑figure invoice.

01 of 04 Market

No real market picture.

You can describe your buyer's pain in fluent detail. You can't tell us, in numbers, whether the market is 3,000 buyers or 300,000 — or which segment is actually pulling demand this quarter.

↳ The cost: a roadmap pointed at a market you've never measured.

02
02 of 04 Competitors

Competitors as anecdotes.

Your read on the field is three landing pages, a Reddit thread, and what an investor said last week. No systematic teardown of pricing, narrative, channels, content cadence, or the complaints their customers keep leaving in reviews.

↳ The cost: you copy the wrong moves and miss the right ones.

03
03 of 04 Gaps

Their gaps stay invisible.

You can see what your rivals are doing. You can't see what they're not doing — the buyer pain they keep ignoring, the channel they're underweight on, the narrative they can't credibly own.

↳ The cost: you fight them on their turf and walk past the door they left open.

04
04 of 04 Plan

Research with no plan.

Even when the data exists, it sits in tabs and decks. No one translates it into the four moves to make on Monday and the two experiments to kill by Friday. Insight without a plan is just a hobby.

↳ The cost: a quarter of reading that never converts to a decision.

03How it works

Three steps. One afternoon.

We deliberately kept this short. The point is to get you back to building — not to keep you stuck in research mode for the third month running.

i.

Brief us in plain English

Paste your site, your deck, or three paragraphs about what you're building and who it's for. You don't need to hand us a competitor list — we surface them ourselves.

~ 5 minutes of your time
ii.

We map the field

Our system pulls market data, demand signals, pricing pages, content libraries, and customer complaints — then synthesizes a market picture and competitor teardown you can argue with.

~ 94 seconds of compute
iii.

You review & act

Edit, sign off, and run the 90‑day plan. We re‑scan the market monthly so your picture stays current, and track outcomes weekly so you can see what's working without writing a report.

04Questions

Sensible objections, answered.

If you don't see yours, write to info@growthkitai.com. A founder, not a form, will reply.

Q.01
Is this an agency, or software?
Software, with operator review. The market map, competitor teardown, and 90‑day plan are produced by our system; a partner from our team checks the synthesis before it lands in your inbox. You get consultancy‑grade output without the consultancy invoice.
+
Q.02
Who is this not for?
Pre‑idea founders looking for an idea to build, and large enterprises with a dedicated market‑intelligence team. We're sharpest for seed and Series A teams that have a product and want to validate the market — and outflank the competition — before they double down.
+
Q.03
How is this different from prompting an LLM yourself?
We've fed the system live market data, pricing pages, competitor content libraries, review‑site complaint mining, and a curated corpus of launch postmortems — then wrapped it in evaluators that catch the shallow, generic takes. The output is tied to artifacts you can act on: a market map, a competitor teardown, a gap analysis, and a 90‑day plan. Not a wall of advice you'd still have to translate.
+
Q.04
What does it cost?
Our basic pilot gives founders limited access for free. Standard plans start at $40/month for the full market map, competitor teardown, gap analysis, and 90‑day plan, refreshed monthly — the kind of engagement a strategy consultancy or investment‑banking team would bill $50k–$150k for. For 1‑to‑1 work with our market‑intel partners, contact us directly.

Q.05
Who is behind it?

/Careers

Build the growth engine from the inside.

We're a small team rebuilding how early-stage companies ship growth. We're hiring two interns who want to do real work, ship real systems, and earn the kind of role most people wait a decade for.

01Why this, why now

An internship, structured like a founding role.

We don't have a coffee-and-spreadsheets track. From week one you'll own a slice of GrowthKit AI's go-to-market — and if you compound, the path forward is the real role, not a referral.

01 — Ownership

Real scope, not shadow work.

You'll ship things that go live, with names attached. No make-work, no decks for the deck pile.

02 — Mentorship

Direct line to founders.

Weekly 1:1s, async feedback in hours not days, and a working style that prizes thinking out loud.

03 — Trajectory

The path is the title.

Strong interns convert into Head of Growth or CMO-track roles. We hire the people we already trust.

04 — Remote

Async, document-first.

Work from anywhere. We optimize for written thinking, deep blocks, and short, sharp meetings.

02Open roles

Two internships. Both lead somewhere big.

01 — Growth Intern · Remote

Growth Intern

Run experiments end-to-end on the real product.

You'll own user acquisition experiments from hypothesis to ship to read-out. Channels, funnels, activation, retention — you'll touch all of it, and write the postmortems that go into the GrowthKit AI corpus.

What you'll do
  • →Design and ship growth experiments across paid, organic, and product surfaces.
  • →Instrument funnels, read the data, and make the call on what to kill or scale.
  • →Build the operating cadence — weekly reviews, kill criteria, dashboards.
  • →Talk to users. Translate what they say into a sharper thesis.
Path forward
A real shot at Head of Growth — the role, the title, the scope.
02 — Marketing Intern · Remote

Marketing Intern

Sharpen the story. Then ship the channels that tell it.

You'll own positioning, narrative, and the content engine that makes GrowthKit AI show up where founders are already reading. From landing pages to launch posts to founder essays — you'll write, ship, and measure.

What you'll do
  • →Write — landing pages, launch sequences, founder posts, lifecycle email.
  • →Own the brand voice across LinkedIn, X, and the GrowthKit AI blog.
  • →Build a content compounding system — not one-offs, a calendar with leverage.
  • →Partner with the growth track to turn experiments into stories worth telling.
Path forward
A real shot at Chief Marketing Officer — the role, the title, the scope.
